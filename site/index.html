Help and sources

Cantus Horarum sets the Liturgy of the Hours to Gregorian chant as the Ordo Cantus Officii (2015) assigns it. It is for private use only.

Using it

  • Choose the hour and the date in the bar; the gear opens the settings: how the psalms are shown, the invitatory, the interface language, the translation, the singing voice, dark mode, the PDF and its print size.
  • ▶ above a score sings it; ■ stops.
  • The small label beside ▶ says where the score comes from: the book and its page, as listed below. Where the score is on GregoBase, tapping the label opens it there.
  • PDF: choose the size and the format, press Make, then Share (on an iPhone: Print, Save to Files) or Download. A booklet prints on both sides, flipped on the short edge, then folded.

說明及資料來源

Cantus Horarum 按照《日課詠唱程序》（Ordo Cantus Officii，2015年）的安排，以額我略聖歌詠唱《時辰頌禱》（拉丁文《日課經》）。只供私人使用。

使用方法

  • 在上方選擇時辰和日期；齒輪按鈕開啟設定：聖詠的顯示方式、序經、介面語言、翻譯、詠唱音域、深色模式、PDF 及其列印大小。
  • 樂譜上方的 ▶ 會唱出樂譜；■ 停止。
  • ▶ 旁邊的小標籤註明樂譜的出處：書名及頁數，見下表。樂譜收錄於 GregoBase 的，點擊標籤即可在該網站開啟。
  • 翻譯：中文取自天主教香港教區禮儀委員會的《每日日課》，只顯示其原文：所唱的拉丁經文與香港日課不同時（例如另一首對經或聖人的讚美詩），不附中文。
  • PDF：選擇大小和格式，按「製作」，然後「分享」（iPhone：列印、儲存到「檔案」）或「下載」。小冊子雙面列印，沿短邊翻頁，再對摺。

Auxilium et fontes

Cantus Horarum Liturgiam Horarum cantu gregoriano exhibet, prout Ordo Cantus Officii (2015) eum disponit. Ad usum privatum tantum.

Usus

  • Horam et diem in summa pagina elige; rota optiones aperit: modum psalmos exhibendi, invitatorium, linguam paginæ, versionem, vocem, modum obscurum, documentum PDF eiusque magnitudinem.
  • ▶ supra cantum eum canit; ■ sistit.
  • Titulus parvus iuxta ▶ indicat unde cantus sumatur: librum eiusque paginam, ut infra. Si cantus in GregoBase invenitur, titulo tacto ibi aperitur.
  • Versiones: Sinica ex Officio cotidiano Commissionis liturgicæ diœcesis Hongcongensis, Anglica ex iBreviary; textus Sinicus tantum ponitur ubi idem est ac Latinus qui canitur.
  • PDF: magnitudinem et formam elige, «Fac» preme, deinde «Communica» vel «Deprome». Libellus in utraque parte imprimitur, in latere breviore versatus, deinde plicatur.

Where the scores come from

Other sources

  • Ordo Cantus Officii, editio typica altera (Libreria Editrice Vaticana, 2015), in the editio cum cantu of antiphonale.net (v.6, 2026): which chant is sung where, and in which mode.
  • GregoBase, the database of Gregorian scores, whose transcriptions are in the public domain (CC0), with antiphonale.net's transcriptions of the Ordo's chants.
  • iBreviary: the Latin texts of the Liturgia Horarum (© Libreria Editrice Vaticana).
  • The Hong Kong Diocesan Liturgy Commission: the Chinese translation (每日日課), and the Chinese interface's terms and names of celebrations.
  • The Nova Vulgata (vatican.va): the division of the psalms into verses.
  • romcal: the General Roman Calendar.
  • Exsurge (Fr Matthew Spencer, MIT) draws the scores; jgabc (Benjamin Bloomfield, public domain) points the psalms to their tones; jsPDF and svg2pdf.js (MIT) make the PDF.
  • EB Garamond (Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo) and Noto Serif TC (Adobe, Google), SIL Open Font License.
  • Visits are counted by GoatCounter (open source): no cookies and nothing personal, only the page, the country, the browser and the screen size.
